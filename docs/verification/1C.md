## What is measured

A **Fitts task**: acquire (point at + click) 40 px targets at varying distances, comparing four factors, in **5-minute blocks**, with a **Borg CR10** exertion rating (0–10) after each supported block:
- **snapping** on vs **raw** (snap radius default 48 px)
- **pinch** (thumb–index tap) vs **dwell** (hold still 600 ms)
- elbow **supported** vs **unsupported**

**You** are the first participant, then **2 more** people.

## Step by step (once the harness exists)

1. **Launch the harness** (exact command provided when I build it — will be a `pnpm --filter @gesture/playground …` page).
2. **Run each condition** as a 5-minute block, cycling the toggles: snapping{on,off} × click{pinch,dwell} × support{supported,unsupported}. The harness logs **median acquisition time** and **click precision** per block.
3. **After each supported block, record your Borg CR10** rating (0 = nothing, 10 = maximal).
4. **Repeat with 2 more participants.**
